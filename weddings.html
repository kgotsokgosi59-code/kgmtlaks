About the business

Hi, we’re KGMTLAKS.

KGMTLAKS PTY-LTD is a photography business based in Gauteng, South Africa. Every shoot begins with listening to the couple, host or tournament organiser so the final gallery feels like their day.

We love the big moments, but we are just as drawn to the small ones: a ring balanced on a heel, a glove on a driver, the first laugh of the morning.

  • Natural, story-led coverage
  • Edited in colour and black & white
  • Every image delivered with the KGM TLAKS signature
